Portfolio Managers Investment Fund Managers Exempt Market Dealers Compliance teams reviewing marketing materials Legal and product teams preparing materials for release

How RegSense works

  1. Step 1 — Upload material
    PDFs, webpages, emails, advertisements, or slide decks are submitted for review.
  2. Step 2 — Identify claims
    The system detects potential performance statements, yield claims, guarantees, comparisons, testimonials, fee statements, and disclosure issues.
  3. Step 3 — Validate against requirements
    Relevant regulatory requirements and internal compliance rules are retrieved and checked using deterministic validation logic.
  4. Step 4 — Produce review-ready output
    RegSense generates structured findings for reviewers (e.g., revise, escalate, or hold), along with policy references, evidence status, and context for decision-making. RegSense does not replace compliance officers.

Business outcome: reduce compliance review time while producing structured, audit-ready evidence for human decision-making.

Built for regulatory defensibility

  • Deterministic compliance checks, not just AI suggestions
  • Verbatim regulatory and policy grounding
  • Audit-ready evidence and traceable review outputs
  • Human-in-the-loop governance with explainable findings

RegSense is designed to help teams move faster without sacrificing compliance rigor, traceability, or reviewer control.
