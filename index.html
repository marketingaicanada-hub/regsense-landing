Why RegSense

  • Append-only audit trail — no silent edits or deletes.
  • Deterministic behavior — stable IDs, idempotent replays, predictable diagnostics.
  • Human-in-the-loop — structured approve/reject decisions with evidence + reason codes.

Who it’s for

Compliance, Legal, and Risk teams that need repeatable reviews with defensible records: financial services, registrants, and any regulated organization.

We’re intentionally starting narrow: a small, reliable workflow that can be audited end-to-end.
