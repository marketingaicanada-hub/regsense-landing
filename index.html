How RegSense Works

  1. Ingest marketing materials (PDFs, slide decks, webpages, emails).
  2. Extract potentially sensitive claims (performance, yield, fees, comparisons, guarantees, endorsements).
  3. Ground the claims in regulatory requirements and internal compliance policy.
  4. Output a structured compliance report with flags, citations, and evidence for a human reviewer.
Documents → Claim Extraction → Policy Mapping → Deterministic Checks → Compliance Report

Why RegSense

  • Deterministic compliance signals take priority over model judgment.
  • Audit-ready evidence is produced by default (quoted text, structured flags, run metadata).
  • Regulatory citations and internal policy references support reviewer verification.
  • Human-in-the-loop workflow: RegSense flags issues; compliance decides disposition.
