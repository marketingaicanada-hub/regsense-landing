RegSense — AI Compliance Engine for Investment Firms

AI-assisted marketing materials review for regulated financial institutions, with structured findings, deterministic checks, and audit-ready outputs for human compliance review.

RegSense helps Canadian investment firms review marketing materials before publication. It detects potential compliance issues such as performance claims, yield language, comparisons, guarantees/assurance tone, fee statements, testimonials/endorsements, and missing or unbalanced risk disclosure. Findings are mapped to applicable regulatory requirements and internal policy checks, then packaged into reviewer-friendly, traceable outputs.

Stage: early MVP. Built to be auditable and reviewer-driven (human-in-the-loop).

Who it’s for

RegSense is designed for:

  • Portfolio Managers (PM)
  • Investment Fund Managers (IFM)
  • Exempt Market Dealers (EMD)
  • Compliance teams and internal legal reviewers
  • Regulated financial institutions operating in Canada

How it works

  1. Document ingestion
    PDFs, slide decks, webpages, emails, and advertisements.
  2. AI-assisted claim extraction
    Extracts candidate marketing claims and key statements for review.
  3. Claim classification
    Performance, yield/distributions, comparisons, guarantees, fees, risk disclosure, endorsements/testimonials.
  4. Policy grounding
    Maps to Canadian regulatory requirements and internal compliance policy sections.
  5. Deterministic validation
    Checks for missing context, prohibited language patterns, and presentation issues.
  6. Structured review output
    Produces audit-ready findings with quoted text, tags, references, and evidence context.

What RegSense outputs

  • Structured flag blocks (quoted text + tags + roll-up)
  • Regulatory + internal policy references (where applicable)
  • Deterministic signals (explicit, replayable)
  • Run metadata and evidence bundles (audit trail)

Positioning: RegSense is decision-support. It does not “approve” marketing.

Built for regulated environments

RegSense is designed for auditability and institutional consistency. Deterministic compliance signals take priority over model judgment. LLMs assist with extraction, explanation, summarization, and drafting — but findings must trace back to deterministic checks, regulatory sources, or clearly labeled internal policy checks.

  • Regulatory source library for Canada (e.g., National Instruments, CSA/OSC guidance)
  • Requirement registry with structured identifiers and references
  • Deterministic validation tools for common marketing risk patterns
  • Evidence layer that preserves quoted text, references, and run metadata
  • Human-in-the-loop controls (reviewer decides; system produces flags + context)
  • Compliance memory concept for institutional consistency (governed, auditable)

Why cloud infrastructure matters

RegSense uses cloud infrastructure to support secure document processing pipelines, event-driven workflows, scalable analysis, and structured evidence outputs.

  • Document processing pipelines (ingestion → extraction → structured output)
  • Event-driven processing and reviewer workflows
  • Secure audit logging and traceable run artifacts
  • Scalable analysis for batch reviews and repeated iterations

AI is applied practically (extraction + explanation), while deterministic checks and sources remain primary.
