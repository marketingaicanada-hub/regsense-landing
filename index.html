Who it’s for

Built for:

  • Portfolio Managers (PM)
  • Investment Fund Managers (IFM)
  • Exempt Market Dealers (EMD)
  • Compliance & Legal teams

How RegSense Works

  1. Claim Detection
    Identifies performance, yield, comparison, and risk-related language in marketing materials.
  2. Regulatory Grounding
    Maps detected claims to applicable Canadian regulatory requirements.
  3. Structured Audit Output
    Produces traceable outputs designed to support compliance review workflows.

Hosted on AWS infrastructure with secure storage and event-driven processing.
